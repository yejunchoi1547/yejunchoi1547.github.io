PCStation Marketing Plan

Sony Group Corporation — Console/PC Hybrid Product Launch Strategy

A comprehensive 14-page marketing plan developed for Sony Group Corporation proposing a new hybrid gaming product, the PCStation, designed to bridge the console and PC gaming markets. The plan covers competitive analysis, SWOT analysis, target market segmentation, growth strategy, pricing, and a full marketing program including social media, omnichannel distribution, and a 1- and 5-year success evaluation framework.

  • Conducted industry and competitor analysis across PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC gaming markets, drawing on Sony's Q2 FY2025 financial data

  • Developed a full marketing program including social media strategy, in-store optimization, and a consumer survey framework to guide product positioning
  • Outlined a quantitative and qualitative success evaluation framework tracking unit sales, revenue, market share, and consumer sentiment across a 5-year window

Professional Project — KISS USA Marketing Internship, Summer 2025

E-Commerce Platform Launch Analysis

KISS USA — Market Feasibility and Recommendation Report

Led an end-to-end e-commerce market feasibility analysis for KISS USA's planned online sales launch of a minimum 1,000 product orders. Researched major platforms including Amazon, analyzed pricing strategies, advertising approaches, and product positioning, then outlined associated costs, growth opportunities, and NPVs for each platform. Identified operational constraints including inventory backlogs and insufficient warehouse staffing that put a large-scale launch at risk, and presented honest recommendations to management — who adjusted the launch scope accordingly.

  • Conducted competitor and pricing analysis across major e-commerce platforms to assess market positioning ahead of the department's online sales launch
  • Met directly with the e-commerce department to evaluate feasibility and align on operational capacity
  • Outlined NPV comparisons and growth projections across platforms and delivered final recommendations to management with full transparency on operational risks
  • Recommendations were accepted and the launch plan was adjusted, demonstrating direct business impact
